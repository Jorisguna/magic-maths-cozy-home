# Art brief: adding more rooms

Goal: more rooms that look like they belong to the same world as the treehouse bedroom, each with enough distinct objects to restore one by one.

## 1. Style (taken from the treehouse bedroom image)
- Warm, cosy storybook fantasy illustration. Hand-painted look with soft ink outlines and rich detail.
- Light: glowing lanterns and fairy lights in gold, against a cool blue-green moonlit forest.
- Rounded, handmade objects: carved wood, patchwork cloth, glass bottles, crystals, climbing vines, small glowing flowers.
- A cut-away view into a giant tree, seen straight on, like a doll's house. The room fills most of the picture and a little forest shows at the sides.
- Gentle and wondrous, never scary.

## 2. Technical requirements for each image
- Landscape, 1408 x 768 pixels (or the largest your tool allows at the same shape; send the largest).
- Straight-on camera with no strong perspective tricks, so objects stay clearly separate.
- At least 22 distinct objects that are easy to point at, with space between them (see the checklist below).
- No text, signs or writing in the picture.
- No characters in front of important objects. A sleeping animal is welcome (it makes a good emotional spell).
- Same lighting and colour mood across rooms, so a world map looks coherent later.
- The picture must look good grey, so contrast between objects matters.

## 3. Object checklist per room
- 8 small things (Sparks, 1 sum each): lanterns, crystals, a candle, a jar of flowers, a small globe, a bell, fairy lights.
- 10 medium things (Incantations, 3 sums each): bed or sofa, shelves, rug, desk, curtain, armchair, plant, hanging herbs, vines, a sleeping animal.
- 3 big moments (Rituals, 6 sums each): a fireplace or stove, a window, the outside view.
- The Great Tree finale reveals everything left over, so bark and background need no list.

## 4. Prompt template (for your image tool)
Attach the treehouse bedroom image as a style reference, then use:

> A cosy fantasy {ROOM} inside a giant tree, cut-away view seen straight on, in the same hand-painted storybook style as the reference image. Warm golden lanterns and fairy lights, a cool moonlit blue-green forest visible at the left and right edges. Rounded handmade furniture, carved wood, glowing crystals and flowers, climbing vines. Contains: {LIST OF 22+ OBJECTS, spread out and clearly separate}. A {SLEEPING ANIMAL} asleep in a cosy spot. Landscape 16:9, highly detailed, no text, no writing, no people.

If the tool changes the style between attempts, ask it to match the reference image again, and keep the same wording for the style sentences each time.

## 5. Room ideas
1. **The potion workshop:** cauldron, bubbling bottles, herb bundles, scales, mortar and pestle, a shelf of jars, a glowing map, a sleeping owl.
2. **The library loft:** bookcases, rolling ladder, reading lamp, a telescope, a globe, a window seat, a sleeping fox.
3. **The kitchen and pantry:** a round stove, hanging pots, jars of jam, a bread oven, a long table with bowls, a teapot, a sleeping dog.
4. **The greenhouse garden:** glowing plants, a watering can, a tiny fountain, mushroom lamps, hanging baskets, a sleeping hedgehog.
5. **The observatory roof:** a big telescope, star charts, a moon window, a hammock, a brass orrery, a sleeping cat.

The small treehouses seen in the background of the bedroom can become doors to these rooms on a world map.

## 6. What happens after you generate an image
1. Pick your favourite and send it to me.
2. I list the restorable objects I can see and propose which are Sparks, Incantations and Rituals.
3. I place the hotspots and send back a check image with each shape outlined. You tell me what to fix.
4. I add the room to the game.
